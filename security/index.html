01Introduction

This policy explains how to report security issues in dinocode, the meetdino.co website and dinocode's sign-in identity at auth.meetdino.co, and how we handle reports.

dinocode runs on your computer and connects to model providers and integrations you choose. We secure the software we write and the small amount of infrastructure we run; we don't control the security of your devices, accounts, networks or third-party services.

02Reporting a security issue

Email security@meetdino.co. Please don't disclose the issue publicly until we've had a reasonable chance to fix it. When you can, include:

  • a description of the issue and its impact
  • the affected version, component or URL
  • steps to reproduce, or a minimal proof of concept
  • relevant logs or output, with secrets removed
  • how to contact you, and whether you'd like public credit

We aim to acknowledge reports within three business days, keep you updated on progress, and coordinate disclosure once a fix is available.

03What's in scope

  • The dinocode software: its local service, terminal UI, CLI, SDKs, permission and trust checks, and handling of credentials and sign-in.
  • meetdino.co and auth.meetdino.co.

Vulnerabilities in third-party model providers, MCP servers or other services should go to those vendors.

04Good-faith research

We won't pursue legal action against good-faith research that follows this policy: test only against your own installation and accounts, avoid privacy violations and disruption, don't access or change other people's data, and give us reasonable time to respond before disclosing.

05How dinocode protects you

  • Commands, credentials and loosened permissions that come from project configuration wait until you trust that project.
  • The agent asks before actions your permission rules don't allow.
  • Sign-in tokens and API keys stay on your computer and are never sent to us.
  • Integration sign-in uses standard OAuth with PKCE, and the sign-in callback only listens on your own machine.

06Your part

Keep your computer and accounts secure, review permissions before granting them, be careful when trusting projects you didn't write, and keep dinocode up to date.

07Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.

08Contact us

Security reports: security@meetdino.co
